Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Morrell Strategy Ltd ("Morrell Strategy", "we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16735963), handles personal information on morrellstrategy.com and in our internal video publishing tool, Morrell uploads. Questions go to admin@morrellstrategy.com.

Our website

When you visit morrellstrategy.com we record the page you visited and the campaign tags in the link you followed (for example utm_source), so we know which of our videos and posts bring people to the site. If you fill in a form or book a call, we keep the details you give us (such as your name and email) to reply to you and run our sales process. We do not sell this information.

Morrell uploads and YouTube API Services

Morrell uploads is an internal tool used only by Morrell Strategy staff to publish videos we produce for our clients to the clients' own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services. By letting us connect a YouTube channel to Morrell uploads, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

When a channel owner or manager signs in with Google and grants access, Morrell uploads receives:

  • an OAuth refresh token for the YouTube upload and channel-management permission the person approved;
  • the connected channel's ID and title;
  • for the channel's own uploads, video titles, descriptions and categories, which we read to match the channel's standing description footer and to avoid uploading the same video twice.

We use this only to upload videos the client has approved, set their title, description and thumbnail, and confirm the upload finished. We do not collect YouTube viewer data, we do not show YouTube content to anyone outside our team, and we do not share or sell any of this information. We do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Refresh tokens and channel settings are stored encrypted in a private storage bucket that only our staff can reach. Video files are streamed from our editing storage to YouTube and are not kept by the tool.

Keeping, deleting and revoking

We keep a channel's token for as long as we publish videos for that channel. When we stop working with a client, or when they ask, we delete the token and channel settings within 7 days. API data we read from YouTube is not kept for more than 30 days unless it is refreshed by a new upload.

You can remove Morrell uploads' access to your YouTube channel at any time from your Google Account's security settings: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. To ask us to delete your data, email admin@morrellstrategy.com.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email admin@morrellstrategy.com and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
